# Find templates on a listing - the Images tab opens on templates suited to the listing, and search offers matching labels (round 2: search above the filter, suggestion row beneath it)

- **Who:** the seller, at a laptop, choosing mockups for one listing.
- **One job:** find the templates that suit this listing, then add its colours.
- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Flow:** open Listing Images → see templates matching the title → search or change labels → hover to preview → open one → click colours into the gallery.
- **Search rules:** search covers every template, by name and label. From two letters, matching labels are offered as filters. A label that would leave nothing is offered as a swap instead.
- **Content:** the 16 real templates and fixture tags from the template-labels scene. The listing is a made-up Christmas tee.
- **Out of scope:** persistence, the API and the Files half of the locator. Previews and gallery tiles show the ivory template photo for every colour, without the design on it.
