**Template positions, round 3 — clickable prototype.** Press **P** and start at frame 1.

- Flip **Adjust position** (frame 1 ↔ 2). On the stage: drag to move, pull a corner to resize, swing the round handle to tilt, arrow keys to nudge. Handles stop at the limits; nothing else reports them.
- **Reset to template** removes the listing's position.
- The **colour-pair** reel tile opens the multiple template (frame 3); the ivory tile comes back.
- Frame 4 is a second entry point: a position left outside the limits by a recalibration.
- Settings is its own flow: [5](goto:template-positions-settings/01-limits) → drag a slider → Review and save → [6](goto:template-positions-settings/02-confirm).
