**Labels live in the search box.** It opens empty. Pills filter by label, and plain text narrows those results by title.

- **Down arrow, Enter:** picks a label from the dropdown and makes it a pill.
- **Enter with nothing highlighted:** keeps your text as a title search.
- **Esc:** closes the dropdown.
- **Backspace in an empty box:** removes the last pill.
- Click the box to see every label, grouped by prefix.

A label that would leave nothing says it replaces the current one. The listing is made up, and previews show the ivory photo without the design. Earlier rounds are on the Archive board.
