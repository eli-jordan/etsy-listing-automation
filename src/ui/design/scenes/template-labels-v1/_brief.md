# Mockup template labels - three directions for labelling and filtering the template library (round 1: A, B and C side by side; A won)

- **Who:** the seller, at a laptop, deciding which mockups suit a design or tidying the library. Unhurried, one person.
- **One job:** find the right mockup templates quickly by what is in the photo.
- **Mode:** Operate. Scanability over expression.
- **Flow:** open Mockup Templates → narrow by labels → open or pick a template. Separately: label a template, or many at once.
- **Content:** the 16 real cc1717 templates. Photos are downscaled from the workspace; the labels on them are fixture guesses from looking at each photo.
- **Labels asked for:** flat lay, male model, female model, multiple people, Christmas, other themes.
- **Out of scope:** persistence, the API, automatic labelling, and the listing editor's template picker. Frames hold state in memory only.
