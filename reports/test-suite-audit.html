Improve confidence by simplifying the suite

The biggest measured opportunity is repeated packaging builds; the most consequential quality defects are tests that pass before behavior happens or when it never happens. Five wheel cases consumed 192.353 seconds, equivalent to 34% of the exploratory hermetic run. Reuse compatible builds, repair weak asynchronous/geometry/cache oracles, and control concurrency explicitly. Move rule matrices and incidental setup to smaller stable interfaces while retaining focused HTTP, browser, filesystem and provider witnesses. Most valuable deletions are bounded duplicates and manual artifact checks; broad workflow deletion would lose idempotency, persistence and boundary protection.

Primary-source research applied to the audit

Test the promise, then choose the smallest sufficient boundary

A useful test names a specific application promise, arranges the minimum necessary state, and rejects a plausible wrong implementation. Stable boundaries here include application operations, Stage/value-object interfaces, Workspace/ListingDocuments, client/provider protocols, HTTP/SSE, component props and accessible output. A cohesive public hook or module can be more durable than a journey through every layer. Exact helper choreography can freeze internals while disconnected wiring stays green. Google’s calculator/factory example shows behavior surviving internal refactors; its payment example replaces several prescribed mock calls with a recorded charge. Test behavior, not implementation and Don’t overuse mocks explain those distinctions.

Do not mechanically ban mocks, private names, goldens or complex setup. No remote writes on unchanged input, unsafe-POST retry prevention, sequential writes, lazy credentials and shared cache identity legitimately use interaction counts. Exact renderer bytes, release contents and architecture edges are intentional contracts. A round-trip can check consistency but needs an independent encoding example when interoperability matters. Google’s change-detector critique targets incidental choreography, not required ordering. HTTPX transports describe the request-level seam; Fowler’s contract-test discussion explains why replayed cassettes alone do not establish today’s provider compatibility.

Push down only when validation is retained. Calling an operation cannot prove route registration, request decoding or HTTP error mapping. Simulated DOM cannot prove layout, actual image decoding or screen-space SVG transforms. Keep focused witnesses for each. Practical pyramid: avoid duplication, FastAPI testing and jsdom’s rendering limits support these distinctions. The proposed application refactors are audit inferences, not savings guarantees from those sources.
