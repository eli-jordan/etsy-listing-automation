# Clean up a mockup photo

Edit the photo `{original}` with your built-in image generation tool.
Open it with your image viewer first so the generator edits that photo rather than inventing a new one.

## The edit

Remove these, and change nothing else:

{removals}

Where something is removed from the shirt, the shirt continues as plain blank fabric: the same colour, folds, shadows, texture and lighting as the fabric around it, with no print, text or graphic left on it. Where something is removed from the background, the background continues naturally.

Keep the person, hair, skin, hands, jewellery, other clothing, background, lighting and framing exactly as they are.

## Output

Save the result as a square PNG at `{output}`, with the same framing as the original. Keep the generator's resolution.

## Check before handing back

Open the result beside the original and confirm:

- nothing listed above remains, not even a faint outline, tint or edge where it was;
- nothing else moved or changed.

Regenerate when a check fails, up to three attempts in all, and keep the best. Reply with one line: the output path, and any check the saved image still fails.
