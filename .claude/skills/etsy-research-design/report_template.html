Rank and bet
My forecast of how well it will sell on Etsy, best first. Strong and good bets have proven demand and room to compete; long shots are missing one of the two.
Weight and shops
How strongly a tag shows up on the top-selling listings, scored by favourites, views and Bestseller or Popular-now badges. A shop's best listing counts in full, its others a quarter. Shops is how many shops use the tag.
Gap
Strong among general best-sellers and missing from the niche: the opening each idea is built on.
Phrase and template
Active Etsy listings matching the idea's exact words, and matching its phrase pattern in the niche. A fresh phrase on an open template is the best case.
