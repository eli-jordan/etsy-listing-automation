## Print requirements

- **Artwork only:** no t-shirt, mockup, model, background scene or frame around the canvas.
- **Background:** transparent if the generator supports it. Otherwise a flat solid colour that appears nowhere in the art, then remove it.
- **Final files:** PNG, transparent background, 4500 x 5400 px (the Comfort Colors 1717 print area), artwork centred horizontally and sitting in the upper part of the canvas. Upscale the generator's output to this size.
- **Printability:** solid opaque colours, no soft glows or semi-transparent edges, no hairlines thinner than ~6 px at final size, at most 4–5 colours unless the style calls for a full illustration.
- **Clean:** no trademarked characters, logos, brand names, real people, song lyrics or film quotes.
