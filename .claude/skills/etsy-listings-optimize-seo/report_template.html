How each term is scored

Shirt searches = eRank's average monthly searches × the share of Etsy's top 100 results that are shirts. Competing tees = T-shirt listings on Etsy matching the term. Reach adds up shirt searches across a listing's sweet-spot, small and busy tags. In Etsy autocomplete means Etsy itself offers the exact phrase, which confirms people search it but says nothing about how often.

Sweet spot
30+ shirt searches a month, up to 12,000 tees. The target.
Small niche
10 to 29 shirt searches, up to 5,000 tees. Real but modest.
Open, unproven
Under 4,000 tees, with little or no measured demand. A cheap bet.
Busy
100+ shirt searches, up to 50,000 tees. Used only where the fit is exact.
Crowded
Fails the test. I only suggest one if it clearly beats the old tag, marked Your call.
Wrong audience
Real searches, but under 15% of the results are shirts.
